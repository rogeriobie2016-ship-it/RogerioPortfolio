Children of the Wind

Director · Scriptwriter · Producer · Editor

A feature documentary about the Cumbe Quilombo, in Aracati, Ceará, one of the first communities in the state to receive a wind farm, built on its land without consultation. It follows the "children of the wind", born to local women and the workers who came to build the parks, against the community's long history of struggle. Clean energy, the film asks: clean for whom?

1 of 3 features picked by Cine Ceará from 100+
